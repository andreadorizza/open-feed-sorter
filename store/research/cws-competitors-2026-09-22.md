# Chrome Web Store competitors, 22 September 2026

Raw notes behind [§6 of LISTING.md](../LISTING.md#6-search). Every figure was
read from the listing page (`https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/<id>?hl=en&ucbcb=1`)
on 22 September 2026, fetched from an EU address. Prices marked **[S]** come
from the developer's own site, **[L]** from the listing text. "Not shown"
means the listing gives no price and no site was checked or found.

User counts on the store are rounded bands, and they move. Treat this as a
snapshot.

How it was checked: listings found through the store's own search (about 45
queries, among them "sort instagram", "sort feed", "tiktok sorter", "instagram
outlier", "sort instagram by views", "free instagram sorter", "open feed
sorter") and web search. Five of them (#1, #2, #3, #7 and #15) were fetched a
second time and matched. All 33 listings were then searched for "open
source", "GitHub" and "MIT licence": **none of them mentions any of the
three.**
