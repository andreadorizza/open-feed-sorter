Open Feed Sorter is installed

Sort any Instagram or TikTok profile by the numbers the grid doesn't show you.

Getting started

  1. Open an Instagram or TikTok profile page.
  2. Click the extension icon and pick a sort and how much to collect.
  3. Press Sort this profile. The tab reloads and scrolls itself while it collects — you can press Stop at any point and keep what it has.
  4. When it finishes, the grid is re-laid-out newest-best-first with every metric on each tile. Re-sort or export from the toolbar above the grid.

Outlier scores

An outlier score compares a post with the account's own recent median, so 3.0x means it did three times the account's normal. Pinned posts and anything under three days old are left out of that median — they'd skew it. Accounts with fewer than 20 eligible posts aren't scored at all.

Everything stays on your machine. There is no account, no telemetry and no backend. The extension reads the same feed responses the page already requested, and exports are generated in the tab.
